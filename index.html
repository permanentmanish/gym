Good To Know

FAQ

Do I need gym experience to join?

No. New members can choose a simple 10–15 minute introduction to the gym, basic machine use and basic safety — or skip it and start on your own if you already know what you're doing.

What are your gym timings?

We're open Monday–Saturday, 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Closed on Sundays.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — you can start with a free trial before committing to a plan. Use the "Start Free Trial" button to see how to get started.

Do you have personal training?

Yes — Personal Training is available as an optional paid monthly package, separate from regular membership.

How do I contact or join?

The fastest way is WhatsApp — tap "WhatsApp Us" and our team will help you out.

Where are you located?

Gandhi Chowk, Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand — see the Visit Us section above for the map and directions.
