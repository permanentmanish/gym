Find the Right Way to Train

You do not need to know everything before you start. Choose the type of training that fits your current goal and experience.

General Fitness

For overall health, mobility and everyday strength — a well-rounded starting point.

Best for: Beginners, or anyone without one specific goal yet.

What to expect: Floor guidance from available trainers, at your own pace.

Strength Training

For building strength, improving technique and becoming more capable with resistance training.

Best for: Beginners, strength-focused users, progressive training.

What to expect: Strength-focused training using resistance equipment, with general floor guidance on form when needed.

Start Free Trial →

Weight Management

For steady, sustainable progress with your weight through consistent training — not quick fixes.

Best for: Anyone focused on long-term, sustainable change.

What to expect: Practical training and floor guidance, scaled to your current fitness level.

Start Free Trial →

Functional Fitness

For movement, coordination, stability, mobility and practical full-body conditioning.

Best for: People who want versatile training and improved everyday movement.

What to expect: Varied, full-body movements — less repetition, more variety.

Training Options

However you're working toward your goal, choose the format that fits.

Open Gym

Flexible access for members who prefer to follow their own workout plan, with normal floor guidance and equipment help from available trainers — not dedicated 1-to-1 coaching.

Personal Training

Optional one-to-one coaching, available as a paid monthly package alongside your membership. Each session runs 45–60 minutes.

₹3,500 / month — PT 8 (8 sessions)

A Simple Approach to Better Training

  • Start Where You Are

    Training should match your current fitness level instead of forcing you to keep up with someone else.

  • Progress With Purpose

    Focus on gradual improvement in strength, fitness, technique and consistency.

  • Train With Confidence

    Clear guidance and a supportive environment help you become more comfortable in the gym.
