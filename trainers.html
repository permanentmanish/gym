What Good Coaching Looks Like

  • Explain, Don't Intimidate

    You should understand what you're doing instead of feeling lost or pressured.

  • Progress Gradually

    Training should become more challenging as you become more capable.

  • Meet You Where You Are

    Your starting point matters. A good coach adapts the guidance to your current ability.

Your First Session Doesn't Have to Be Perfect.

If you are new to gyms, the goal is not to know everything on day one. Start with the basics, ask questions, and build confidence one session at a time.
